🕹️ Dedicated Buttons & 📡 BLE Wi-Fi Provisioning Hub

▼

Your dual mechanical switches (Left D25 & Right D26) provide intuitive, zero-latency control over the displays and active page functions.

⬅️ Left Switch (Pin D25) : Page Cycle

  • Single Click: Cycle forward through 13 TFT screens (P1 -> P13).
  • Double Click: Toggle OLED display mode (HUD, Clocks, Air Quality, Animations, WiFi info).
  • Long Hold (>700ms): Toggle auto-carousel on/off.

➡️ Right Switch (Pin D26) : Page Action

  • Pomodoro Page (P3): Click toggles Start/Pause; Double Click resets timer!
  • Climate Page (P6): Click cycles through 7 unique climate themes!
  • Watchface Page (P7): Click cycles through all 15 custom watchface styles!
  • Other Pages: Click cycles OLED animation themes or TFT color palettes!

📡 BLE Nordic Wi-Fi Credential Provisioning

Connect using any BLE Terminal app (service 6e400001-b5a3-f393-e0a9-e50e24dcca9e) to configure Wi-Fi wirelessly:

  • Send WIFI:YourSSID:YourPassword over BLE to save credentials and restart!
  • Send STATUS to verify network connection state and obtain IP address.
