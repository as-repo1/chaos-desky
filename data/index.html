⌨️ Dual Mechanical Switches & 📡 BLE UART Telemetry

▼

Your hardware hub features resistorless dual mechanical switches (Left D25 & Right D26) with manual navigation by default and open Nordic UART BLE telemetry!

⬅️ Left Key (Pin D25 ➔ GND) — OLED Controls & To-Do / Notes Jump

Controls your Left Display (OLED) and gives instant productivity shortcuts:

  • Single Click: Switches pages/modes directly on the OLED Display (HUD ➔ Big Clock ➔ Sparkline ➔ Marquee ➔ Cyber Cat)!
  • Double Click: Instantly jumps to the To-Do List (Page 4) and Notes / Logs (Page 6) screens on the TFT! Toggles back and forth on each double-click!
  • Long Press (>700ms): Quick-jumps to Pomodoro screen and toggles timer Work/Pause (Customizable via power-user options)!

➡️ Right Key (Pin D26 ➔ GND) — TFT Navigation & Watchface Sync

Controls your Right Display (TFT) and syncs time displays across screens:

  • Single Click: Manually switches pages directly on the TFT Display!
  • Double Click: Switches the OLED display to the Big Clock Face, jumps the TFT to Watchface Studio, and switches between multiple iconic watch faces (Swiss, Nixie, Casio F-91W, and Casio G-Shock)!
  • Long Press (>700ms): Instant hotkey to snap straight to your iconic Casio F-91W watchface and cycle themes!

📡 BLE Nordic UART & PC Telemetry Receiver

Open any Bluetooth Terminal app on iOS/Android or desktop script and connect to chaos-desky without OS encryption pairing errors!

  • Type casio or gshock to switch watch faces wirelessly!
  • Type page <0..9> to jump to specific telemetry screens!
  • Send pc:CPU=52C, GPU=61C to broadcast real-time PC gaming telemetry directly onto your OLED & TFT displays!
